# Business model

KABADI+ aligns collector income with recycler supply efficiency. Revenue layers are recycler SaaS, a configurable transaction fee, enterprise/EPR workflow services, pooled logistics and aggregated intelligence APIs. The admin business page computes demo GMV, fee revenue, subscriptions, logistics and contribution margin from network records.

The defensibility loop is network participation → transactions → price history → recycler performance data → material intelligence → better matching → stronger participation. No government approval or certification is claimed.
