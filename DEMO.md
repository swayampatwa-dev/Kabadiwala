# Five-minute SIH demo

1. Sign in as collector. Show earnings, formally routed kilograms, Hindi/Marathi switching and voice guidance.
2. Open **Scan Scrap**, choose a PCB image, run the deterministic AI demo, explain confidence/advisory disclosure and fair-price reasons.
3. Use the result to create a 12.5 kg PCB lot. Open its QR passport and highlight the public-safe projection.
4. Show recycler matches and why the top match wins. Switch to recycler, submit a quote, then demonstrate handover OTP `123456`, final weight `9.8 kg`, and the resulting 21.6% high-priority discrepancy.
5. Record a local UPI payment. Return to passport/earnings, then switch to admin to show transaction, anomaly, dataset and formalization intelligence updates.

Offline proof: collector enables Offline Demo Mode, creates a lot, reloads, disables offline mode and syncs the IndexedDB queue. Repeat sync to explain server idempotency.

Price fairness proof: call anomaly check with benchmark ₹410/kg and offer ₹180/kg; the service flags a 56.1% deviation.
